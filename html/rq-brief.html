MorNING Experimental Research Questions

A bilingual roadmap for explaining how the experimental section supports the TMMA motivation, the prompting design, deployment robustness, and representation-level re-balancing.

Purpose Use RQs as the reader-facing logic of the experiment section, not as a list of figures.
RQ1

Establish the TMMA challenge and MorNING's effectiveness.

RQ2

Explain which prompting designs make MorNING work.

RQ3

Stress-test MorNING under realistic deployment missing patterns.

RQ4

Verify whether re-balancing bridges the representation shift.

Bilingual RQ Design With Experiments

RQ1

Effectiveness Under TMMA

English
Why do existing adaptation methods struggle under TMMA, and can MorNING effectively address this challenge?

中文
为什么现有适应方法在 TMMA 下会失效，而 MorNING 是否能够有效解决这一挑战？

Corresponding Experiments

  • Main experiments under TMMA.
  • Comparison with TTA methods.
  • Comparison with prompt tuning methods.
  • Comparison with traditional multimodal missing-learning methods.
RQ2

Prompting Design Analysis

English
Which prompting designs are critical to MorNING, and how do they affect test-time re-balancing?

中文
MorNING 中哪些提示设计是关键的，它们如何影响测试时的重平衡过程？

Corresponding Experiments

  • Ablation study of MorNING components.
  • Prompt length and prompt position analysis.
  • Prompt construction mechanism comparison.
RQ3

Robustness in Realistic Deployment

English
Can MorNING remain reliable under realistic deployment scenarios with extreme, dynamic, or episodic modality missing?

中文
在真实部署中面对极端、动态或周期性的模态缺失时，MorNING 是否仍然可靠？

Corresponding Experiments

  • Extreme missing-rate robustness.
  • Random missing type per batch, i.e., non-IID online missing patterns.
  • Episodic disappearance of one modality over consecutive stream intervals.
RQ4

Re-Balancing and Distribution Alignment

English
Does the re-balancing prompting mechanism in MorNING bridge the representation-level distribution shift caused by test-time modality missing?

中文
MorNING 的重平衡提示机制是否桥接了测试时模态缺失造成的表示层分布偏移？

Corresponding Experiments

  • t-SNE representation visualization.
  • MMD analysis of source-target representation discrepancy.

Suggested narrative: RQ1 motivates the need for MorNING, RQ2 explains the mechanism, RQ3 verifies deployment robustness, and RQ4 validates the representation-level re-balancing effect.
